## Hi, I'm Eyob 👋

I'm a software engineer who likes building useful things with **Python and Django**.

Most of my work is on backend systems, APIs, and increasingly **authentication and identity**, including SSO, SAML, OAuth/OIDC, Auth0, MFA, and the occasional *"why is this SSO flow doing that?"* debugging session.

Lately, I've also been spending a lot of time with **AI, LLMs, and AI agents**, both for building products and for finding better ways to build software.

## Things I enjoy working on

- 🐍 Python, Django & backend systems
- 🔐 Authentication, SSO & identity
- 🤖 AI agents & automation
- ☁️ Docker, Kubernetes & cloud infrastructure
- 🚀 SaaS products and random ideas that somehow turn into side projects
- 📈 Trading tools and market-related experiments
